# Demie Photography — Content Checklist

This is everything we need from Demie Photography to replace the placeholder template
content and finish the website. Items are ordered by priority, with **Photos first**.

> **Before you start:** the template ships with ~26 homepage styles and ~20 gallery
> layouts. We only keep the ones you choose. The quantities below assume **one homepage,
> one gallery page, and the standard About / Services / Blog / Contact pages**. We'll
> trim the list once you pick your homepage and gallery style.

---

## 1. Photos (highest priority — site cannot launch without these)

### File requirements (apply to every photo)
- **Resolution:** at least **2000px on the longest side** (3000px+ preferred for full-width hero/gallery).
- **Format:** JPG (quality 80–90) or PNG. No watermarks.
- **File naming:** `category-description.jpg` (e.g. `wedding-bride-groom.jpg`). No spaces or special chars.
- **Delivery:** a Google Drive / Dropbox / WeTransfer link, organised into folders that match the sections below.
- **Rights:** we need a note confirming you own the photos and have permission to publish any
  recognisable people (model release). This matters for weddings/portraits.
- **Orientation notes:** "Landscape" = wider than tall; "Portrait" = taller than wide.

### A. Hero / Homepage slider — **3 landscape images**
Large, dramatic "hero" shots that fill the screen at the top of the homepage.
- 3 of your absolute best images (wedding, portrait, or event).
- Landscape, high resolution, subject not too close to the edges (text overlays on top).
- Give each a short label, e.g. `1. Bride & Groom`, `2. Portrait`, `3. Event`.

### B. Portfolio / Gallery — **the biggest request, ~24–40 images total**
This is the core of the site. Organise by service category and send **6–12 per category**.
Pick the categories you actually offer (see Services in section 2), for example:

| Category | How many | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weddings | 8–12 | mix of wide venue shots + close-ups |
| Portraits / Personal portfolio | 6–10 | individual + editorial style |
| Events | 6–10 | corporate, parties, ceremonies |
| Studio / Newborn / Family | 6–10 | studio look |
| Drone / Cinematography stills | 4–8 | aerial, landscape |
| Wildlife (if offered) | 4–8 | |
| Behind the scenes | 3–5 | you working, gear, studio |

- Include a **title + one-line caption** for each (e.g. "Wedding — Blantyre, 2024").
- Mark your **3–5 absolute favourites** — these go on the homepage portfolio section.

### C. About page
- **2–3 studio/team candid shots** — you at work, the studio space, gear.
- **1 portrait of the founder/lead photographer** (for the "About Demie" section).

### D. Team — **1 headshot per person**
- A clean, well-lit portrait (can be casual, on-brand) for each team member listed in section 4.
- Ideally 1–6 people. Square or portrait crop works.

### E. Testimonials — **1 headshot per reviewer (optional but recommended)**
- Small client headshot for each testimonial (section 3). If unavailable, we'll use a generic avatar.

### F. Blog — **1 image per post**
- One relevant image per blog article (section 5). Landscape, ~1200px+.

### G. Instagram feed — **6 square images**
- The site shows an Instagram gallery in the slide-out menu. Send 6 recent favourite square posts.

### H. Awards — **1 image per award (optional)**
- If you have award certificates / trophy photos, send them (section 7). Otherwise we'll remove this section.

### I. Shop products — **1 image per product**
- Only if you sell prints/frames. Product photo on a clean background for each item (section 8).

### J. Background / texture images
- 1 wide, dark, low-detail image we can use behind page headers and footer sections
  (e.g. a blurred venue or texture). Optional — the template has defaults we can keep.
