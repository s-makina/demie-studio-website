About Demie Photography

Demie Photography is a photography studio based in Chilomoni, Blantyre, serving couples, families and brands across Malawi.

From weddings and portraits to events and drone cinematography, our team captures the moments that matter with care, creativity and a personal touch. Hire Demie Photography for your next event.
