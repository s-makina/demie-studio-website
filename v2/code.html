Featured Love Story

A Love Story in Blantyre

Victoria & Alexander • The Berkshires Estate

Set against the gilded architecture of an early 20th-century manor, Victoria and Alexander celebrated a weekend of quiet intimacy and grand evening revelry. Our team documented every chapter over three days — from the welcome aperitivo on the terrace to the torchlit dance floor under the summer stars.

Shot interchangeably across vintage 35mm film stock and crisp medium-format digital cameras, the resulting archive pairs nostalgic film texture with luminous modern clarity.

“Looking back at our gallery felt like stepping right back into the perfume of the gardens and the sound of our friends cheering.”
